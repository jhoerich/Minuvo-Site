Privacy Policy

for the iPhone app Minuvo and this website · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This is a translation. If the two versions differ, the German version applies.

In short: Minuvo processes everything on your iPhone only. There is no server, no user account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no sharing with third parties. We do not receive any data from the app.

No data protection officer has been appointed, as this is not required by law.

2. Principle: your data stays on your device

Minuvo has no server of its own and does not connect to us or to third parties over the internet. The app contains no libraries for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking. Everything the app stores lives in the app's protected storage on your iPhone (the app's own container and an app group that only Minuvo and its extensions can read). We as the developer have no access to it.

3. What the app processes on your iPhone

3.1 Screen Time

Minuvo uses Apple's Screen Time API (Family Controls, DeviceActivity, ManagedSettings) so that you can limit apps on your own iPhone. iOS asks for your permission once.

  • Your selection of apps, categories and websites is stored by iOS as encrypted tokens. Minuvo cannot read app names or content from them.
  • Usage times are displayed inside the app by an Apple extension. Minuvo itself only learns whether a limit you chose has been reached (for example "budget used up") and does not store individual usage minutes.
  • Minuvo never sees what you do in other apps or what content you view.

3.2 Health (steps only, read-only)

If you allow it, Minuvo reads your daily step count from Apple Health to credit you extra time. Only steps measured by your iPhone or Apple Watch count, not manually entered ones. Minuvo writes nothing to Health and reads no other Health data. The step count is stored on your device only and is never used for advertising or shared.

3.3 Data you enter in the app or that is created there

  • your first name or nickname for the greeting (optional),
  • settings such as daily budget, quiet hours, limits for single apps and notifications,
  • your time account and history: earned and taken minutes, sessions, reading times, days within budget, progress,
  • a technical diagnostics log (at most 500 entries) that helps to find bugs.

This data only leaves your iPhone if you share it yourself, for example if you send us the diagnostics log via the share sheet (see section 5).

3.4 Notifications

If you allow notifications, Minuvo schedules them locally on your iPhone (for example "budget used up"). There are no push notifications from a server.

3.5 Legal basis

As this processing takes place only on your device and we receive no data, nothing is transferred to us. To the extent that the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies nonetheless, the processing serves to provide the app's features to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For the step count as health data, the legal basis is your explicit consent in iOS (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR); you can withdraw it at any time in the iOS Settings.

4. Apple

Minuvo is distributed via Apple's App Store and TestFlight. Apple processes its own data in that context, for example when you download the app. If you agreed in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with anonymised crash reports and usage statistics; feedback sent via TestFlight (such as screenshots and comments) also reaches us through Apple. Apple is responsible for this. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message (and any diagnostics log you attach) to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the message once it has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time, the page requested and browser details in order to deliver the page and protect it against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses for this. Details: GitHub's privacy statement.

This website sets no cookies, contains no analytics or tracking tools and loads no content from other servers. The font is hosted on this website itself.

7. Retention and deletion

  • In the app, Profile → "Reset Everything" resets the stored data (while a block is active, this is only possible afterwards).
  • If you delete Minuvo, iOS removes all of the app's data and its restrictions.
  • You can revoke access to Screen Time, Health and notifications at any time in the iOS Settings.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). As we receive no data from the app, this usually only concerns emails you have sent us.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live or the one responsible for us (Art. 77 GDPR).

9. Children

Minuvo is intended for adults using their own iPhone. It is not a parental-control app.

10. Changes

If the app changes in a way that processes other data, we will update this policy and the date above.
